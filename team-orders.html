The Process

How team orders work

  1. 1

    Tell us about your team

    Share your team info, rough quantity, design ideas, and deadline.

  2. 2

    Get mockups & a quote

    We send design options with per-item and bulk pricing. We'll help you collect sizes, too!

  3. 3

    Approve & pay deposit

    Approve the final design, pay a 50% deposit, and we get to work.

Plan ahead! After design approval, orders typically take about 1–2 weeks for apparel and 2–4 weeks for shoes, embroidery, and hand-painted items. Larger orders may need extra time, so reach out early.
