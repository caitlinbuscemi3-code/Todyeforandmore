Ordering

How do custom orders work?

It's three simple steps:

  1. Describe your idea using our custom order form. We'll email you afterward to collect any photos or inspiration.
  2. Get mockups & pricing. We'll send design options with pricing for each.
  3. Approve & pay your deposit. Once you love your design, a 50% deposit gets your order started.
What's the difference between "Signature Designs" and "Made to order"?

Signature Designs are our own designs that you can add straight to your cart. Each one is made fresh when you order, and every item shows about how long it takes to ship.

Made to order items are designed just for you. You'll request one, approve a mockup, and then we'll make it.

Can I get one of your signature designs in a different team, name, or color?

Yes! Tap "Customize This Design" on any signature design and tell us what you'd like changed. Customized versions of our signature designs are the same price as the original.

I don't see what I'm looking for. Can you make it?

Just ask! Some of our favorite projects started with "Can you make…?" Send us a message or start a custom request.

Do you take team, group, and wedding orders?

Absolutely. We make matching gear for teams, schools, companies, groups, events, and bridal parties. Team and group orders start at 6 pieces, and bridal parties of any size are welcome. Request a quote on our Team & Bulk Orders page.

What sizes do you offer? How do they fit?

Our t-shirts, crewnecks, and hoodies are unisex sizes, XS–4X (2X–4X add $3). Unisex sizing fits like a relaxed men's fit, so if you like a more fitted look, you may want to size down.

Kids items come in kids sizes, from 12 months to Youth XL. Exact measurements vary by style, so if you're between sizes, message us and we'll help you choose. Please double-check your size before ordering, since all sales are final.

Pricing & Payment

How much do custom pieces cost?

Every piece is different, so your exact price depends on the design, size, and details. You can see starting prices in our pricing guide, and you'll always get a confirmed price with your mockup before you pay anything.

Does the custom shoe price include the shoes?

No. Shoe customization starts at $75, and the cost of the shoes is added on top of that.

How do I get my shoes to you?

You can send us your own pair or have us buy a new pair for you. If you're sending your own, we'll share the shipping details once your mockup is approved. If we buy them, the cost of the shoes is added to your total.

How does the deposit work?

Once you approve your mockup, we'll collect a 50% deposit to start your order. The remaining balance is due when your order is ready, before it ships.

Because we buy materials and start creating right away, deposits are non-refundable once work begins.

How is bulk and team pricing handled?

Bulk pricing depends on the products, design, and quantity, so every team order gets its own quote. Request a team quote.

Timing

How long will my order take?

Timing depends on what you order. After your mockup is approved and your deposit is in, most orders take about:

  • Apparel: 1–2 weeks
  • Shoes, embroidery & hand-painted items: 2–4 weeks

Larger group orders may need a little extra time.

I need it by a certain date. Can you make it happen?

Tell us your date in your request and we'll let you know honestly whether we can make it. The earlier you reach out, the better!

What's the holiday order deadline?

To get your order in time for Christmas, please place your order by December 5. For custom orders, the earlier you send your request, the better, since your mockup needs to be approved before we start.

How soon will I hear back about my request?

We reply to custom and team requests within 2–3 business days with mockups and pricing.

Shipping

Where do you ship?

We ship within the United States only.

How much are shipping and tax?

Flat $8 shipping. Sales tax calculated at checkout.

How will my order ship? Will I get tracking?

Orders ship with USPS, UPS, or FedEx, depending on the package and where it's going. As soon as your order ships, you'll get an email with your tracking number. Delivery usually takes a few business days after it ships.

Where's my order?

Every piece is made when you order, so check the ship time listed on your item (most take about 1–2 weeks, and embroidered, beaded, and hand-painted pieces about 2–4 weeks). Once it ships, we'll email your tracking number.

Haven't seen it? Check your spam folder, then contact us with your order number and we'll look into it.

My package was lost or stolen. What should I do?

Please check with your neighbors and your local post office first, then contact us. Once a package is marked delivered, we aren't able to replace it, but we'll gladly help you work with the carrier.

Policies

All sales are final. Every piece is made to order or made in small batches, so we're not able to accept returns, exchanges, or cancellations. Please review your mockup, sizes, and spelling carefully before approving.

Can I return or exchange my order?

All sales are final, including signature designs. We're not able to accept returns or exchanges, including for sizing, so please check your size before ordering.

Can I cancel or change my order?

You can make changes or cancel anytime before you approve your mockup, at no cost. Once your mockup is approved and your deposit is paid, we start working on your order and it can't be canceled or refunded.

Who checks the spelling and details on my mockup?

We'll always do our best to catch mistakes, but approving your mockup means you've confirmed the spelling, names, numbers, colors, and sizes. Please look it over carefully, since your order is made exactly as approved.

What if my order arrives damaged or incorrect?

If your order arrives damaged, or doesn't match your approved mockup, please contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos. If the mistake is ours, we'll make it right.

Will my piece look exactly like the photos?

Every piece is handmade, so small variations in color, placement, stitching, and paint are part of what makes yours one of a kind. Colors may also look a little different on different screens.

What if I send you my own item to customize?

We love working on your shoes, jackets, and other items! Please send them clean and in good condition. Because every item is different, we can't be responsible for existing wear, defects, or how a material reacts to paint or embroidery.

Care

How should I wash my custom apparel?
  • Printed (vinyl) & embroidered apparel: wash inside out in cold water on a gentle cycle, and tumble dry low or hang dry. Don't iron directly on the design.
  • Beaded embroidery: hand wash cold or spot clean, and lay flat to dry.
  • Hand-painted jackets: spot clean only.
  • Hand-stitched sweaters: hand wash cold and lay flat to dry.
How do I care for my hand-painted shoes?

Spot clean with a soft, damp cloth. Don't put them in the washing machine, and avoid soaking them. Keeping them out of heavy rain helps your design last.
