The Process

How it works

Custom work is personal, so there's no "Buy Now" here. We work with you one-on-one.

  1. 1

    Describe your idea

    Fill out the request form below. After you submit, we'll email you to collect any photos or inspiration.

  2. 2

    Get mockups & pricing

    Within a few days you'll receive design mockup options with pricing for each.

  3. 3

    Approve & pay deposit

    Pick your favorite, pay a 50% deposit, and we start creating. The balance is due when it's ready.

How long does it take? Once your mockup is approved and your deposit is in, most orders take about 1–2 weeks for apparel and 2–4 weeks for shoes, embroidery, and hand-painted items. Need it by a certain date? Tell us in your request.
