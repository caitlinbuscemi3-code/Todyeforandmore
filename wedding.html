Plan Ahead

When should I order?

We recommend reaching out at least 6–8 weeks before your wedding, and earlier for large bridal parties or if you'd like shoes and apparel.

  1. 1

    Tell us your plans

    Share your date, colors, names, and what you'd like. We reply within 2–3 business days with mockups and pricing.

  2. 2

    Approve & pay deposit

    Once you love the designs, a 50% deposit gets your order started.

  3. 3

    We make it

    Apparel takes about 1–2 weeks. Shoes, embroidery, and hand-painted pieces take about 2–4 weeks.

  4. 4

    Ready for the big day

    We ship with tracking (US only), so everything arrives with time to spare.
