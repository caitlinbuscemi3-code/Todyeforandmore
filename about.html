Our Story

Handmade with love in Metro Detroit

Hi there! I'm Caitlin, the maker behind To Dye For and More. I'm from the Detroit, Michigan area, and creating is my happy place. Nothing makes me happier than taking a spark of inspiration and turning it into something you can wear, hold, and show off.

Whether you're treating yourself or hunting for the perfect gift for someone special, I'll work with you to bring your idea to life, from the very first sketch to the final stitch (or brushstroke!).

I do all things shoes, embroidery, hand painting, and more. And if you don't see what you're looking for? Just ask! Some of my favorite projects started with "Can you make…?"
